Chapter 01 / 37

How the module works

Who uses thisAll users
OutcomeUnderstand the sequence and the difference between operational and payroll states.

The module preserves recorded punches, builds an expected employee-day from approved setup, and asks the appropriate people to review exceptions. Importing a CSV is the start of processing, not the final attendance approval.

StageMain actionResult
1 SetupReview roster, attendance policy, assignments and calendarsAn employee has an unambiguous schedule and reviewed rules for the work date.
2 CaptureUpload and preview CSV; generate expected attendanceRecorded punches and no-punch days can be compared with expectations.
3 ResolveCorrect mappings or timestamps; obtain approved employee correctionsExceptions retain a reason and a trace to original evidence.
4 ImportReview import and confirm the ready groupsNative attendance batches and punches are saved as drafts.
5 ReviewEmployee or authorized HR submits; supervisor approves or returnsAttendance review decision is recorded separately from document status.
6 Prepare payReview overtime and regular payable quantitiesApproved quantities have separate HR decisions and references.
7 CloseResolve readiness blockers and close the attendance periodA controlled snapshot protects the reviewed period.
8 HandoffStage payroll incidences and obtain independent checkingNative incidences are available for the normal payroll workflow.

What the statuses do not mean

  • Preview saves or refreshes review information; it does not create payable attendance.
  • Imported means punches were saved. The native document can still be Draft and unprocessed.
  • Supervisor approved records acceptance of the attendance evidence. It does not complete payroll documents.
  • Closed period protects attendance decisions. Payroll handoff and incidence approval follow separately.
  • Completed payroll incidence is an input to payroll. It is not proof that a payroll run was calculated, finalized or paid.
Completion checkAlways verify the status at the stage you completed. Do not use one green count or one approval as evidence that the entire chain has finished.

Access setup checklist

  1. Confirm the user is active and has active membership of the selected role.
  2. Grant the required form, process, window and table access. A visible menu alone does not grant its underlying record access.
  3. Grant the intended organization access, including write access where the action creates or changes records. Check both the HR employee and linked business partner organization.
  4. Link each self-service user to the correct employee/business partner. Verify the employee user’s Supervisor.
  5. Configure the authorized HR and payroll role lists using role IDs. Apply record restrictions for employee and supervisor access.
  6. After role or dictionary changes, reset the application cache as appropriate, sign out, sign in again with the intended role, and reopen the form.

Approval identity

A supervisor decision must be made by the assigned supervisor or a valid approved delegate. Self-approval is blocked. HR administrator access does not automatically make an administrator the employee or the assigned supervisor. A second HR reviewer is required for the protected maker/checker decisions.

Completion checkTest with an employee account, a supervisor account and an HR account. Confirm each can perform its intended action and cannot view or decide unrelated employee records.

Chapter 04 / 37

First time setup sequence

Who uses thisHR setup owner and independent reviewer
OutcomeMake a controlled population ready for attendance processing.
  1. Prepare employee records, employment dates, organization, work group, department, employee code, user link and supervisor.
  2. Create or verify Work Group, Shift Group and Work Shift. Add the company holiday calendar and reviewed holiday classifications.
  3. Create an Attendance Roster with a fixed week or a rotation, break intervals, timezone and punch boundaries.
  4. Create the Attendance Policy and record the company decisions for grace, rounding, breaks, payable units and other applicable rules.
  5. Approve configuration versions in Attendance Assignment Review using an independent HR reviewer.
  6. Create effective-dated Roster Assignments, Policy Assignments and Employment Legal Regime Assignments. Review and approve them.
  7. Provide approved delegations or fallback routes where required. Confirm employee and supervisor access.
  8. Verify an attendance document type and its numbering, and the operator’s organization access.
  9. Run assignment, supervisor and legal coverage reports for a small date range.
  10. Generate expected attendance for that range. Resolve setup conflicts before the first live CSV import.

Recommended first pilot

Use a small, known employee group and one completed workday. Verify a normal workday, a rest day, a holiday, an approved leave day, a missing punch and a person with no punches. Expand only after the expected results and approval routes are understood.

Attendance policy and leave policy are different

A leave policy controls leave eligibility, credit and leave requests. An attendance policy controls the reviewed attendance rules and payable-unit decisions. The attendance roster provides the work schedule. Assigning a leave policy does not, by itself, finish attendance setup. Older preview paths may mention a leave-policy assignment; check the active attendance roster/policy configuration as well as the leave assignment needed for leave integration.

Completion checkCoverage reports should show a unique applicable schedule and policy for each employee/date. A checkbox marked Active is not a substitute for independent review or date coverage.

Each shift spans eight hours with seven net working hours and one proposed unpaid break. Five teams rotate through Day → Evening → Night → Off → Off, with phased starting points. Chapter 07 / 37

Work groups shifts and calendars

Who uses thisHR setup owner
OutcomeMaintain the reusable schedule and calendar building blocks.

Work Group and Shift Group

Use Work Group for employees who share scheduling rules. Shift Group and its schedules remain supporting master data; the effective Attendance Roster and its reviewed assignments determine the modern attendance expectation. Keep the employee’s group and the assigned roster consistent.

Work Shift

  1. Create or open the intended shift. Give it a clear name and search key.
  2. Set the shift times and the organization according to the company schedule.
  3. For the SLERPTECH office example, the span is 08:30–17:30. Record the one-hour lunch in the roster break configuration; do not represent nine elapsed hours as nine payable work hours.
  4. Save and use this shift in the fixed roster or appropriate rotation lines.
  5. Validate an overnight shift through the roster workday boundary and punch window, including next-day end time.

Human Resource Calendar: payroll years and periods

  1. Open Human Resource Calendar in the SLERPTECH tenant and select SHRP_LK_HR — SLERPTECH Human Resource Calendar. Organization * makes this a shared tenant calendar.
  2. Open Year, select 2026 and verify the payroll and linked financial year. The prepared year has Start Date 1 January 2026, Quantity 12 and Net Days 30. Net Days is the configured payroll basis, not the number of days in every month.
  3. Open Period and verify all twelve monthly periods: January through December, correct start/end dates and no overlap. The company package fills missing months and preserves the existing October–December entries.
  4. Use the same HR calendar for the company payroll year across the three shift teams. Opening or closing accounting periods and executing payroll are separate operations.
  5. Prepare the next year explicitly before using dates outside 2026; this release does not create a 2027 HR year.

Holiday calendar: attendance non-working dates

  1. Maintain actual holiday dates in the holiday calendar referenced by Attendance Roster. The prepared three-shift rosters reference SLERPTECH LK Office Holidays 2026. This is separate from HR Calendar > Year > Period.
  2. Verify dates and applicability against the approved company source. Create Attendance Holiday Classifications with a source reference and work group where needed.
  3. Have an independent authorized reviewer approve the classifications in Attendance Assignment Review and review the roster calendar reference.

Supported holiday labels include Poya, statutory, bank-only, company and other. A bank-only label does not automatically make the date a non-working day for every employee. Holiday classification is evidence for the company’s reviewed treatment; it does not calculate holiday pay.

Completion checkGenerate expectations for a known workday, a weekend/rest day and a known holiday. Confirm the displayed classification matches the assigned roster and calendar.

Chapter 09 / 37

Roster cycles breaks and overnight work

Who uses thisHR schedule owner
OutcomeModel complete rotations and verify work that crosses midnight.

Build every cycle day

For a rotation, specify a cycle start and a cycle length, then add a Cycle Line for every day offset. Offset 0 is the first cycle day. Mark each offset as work or rest and choose a shift for workdays. A seven-day cycle needs offsets 0 through 6; a rest day still needs its line. The current implementation supports cycles up to 42 days.

  1. Save the roster parent before entering Cycle Lines.
  2. Add each offset once, starting at zero and ending at cycle length minus one.
  3. Select Is Work Day and the intended shift on working offsets. Enter explicit rest-day lines for the remaining offsets.
  4. Check a date in the second repetition of the cycle, not only the first week.
  5. Review the roster version and its effective assignment before generating employee-days.

Overnight example

For overnight work, define the schedule and workday boundary so an end punch after midnight belongs to the shift’s work date. Review applicable night-work requirements and effective dates. Keep both sides of midnight in the same resolved shift evidence.

For example, a reviewed shift that starts on Monday evening and ends on Tuesday morning should retain actual timestamps on both dates, while belonging to the resolved Monday workday. Do not replace the Tuesday end date with Monday to make the CSV fit. Review the maximum shift length and before/after punch windows for this schedule.

Break Intervals child tab

The main roster supplies the first scheduled break. Use Break Intervals for additional numbered breaks, with an offset from shift start and duration. Keep breaks ordered, within the shift and non-overlapping. Expected punch counts depend on the resolved schedule and break configuration; four punches are the office example, not a universal rule.

CheckExpected outcome
All cycle offsets presentEvery day resolves to work or rest without an omitted cycle line.
Midnight crossingEnd punches remain on their true date and map to the intended workday.
Breaks inside shiftNo overlap, negative duration or interval outside the scheduled span.
Review dates cover the dayApplicable company/night-work reviews and assignment dates remain effective.
Completion checkReviewed configuration is protected. Create a new version for a change, then create the required effective assignments. Check the resulting employee-day before using it for import.

Chapter 11 / 37

Assignments legal coverage and routing

Who uses thisHR maker and independent reviewer
OutcomeGive each employee an effective schedule, policy and approval route.

Roster and policy assignments

  1. Open Roster Assignments or Policy Assignments and create a draft.
  2. Select the assignment type, employee or work group as applicable, and the reviewed roster/policy version.
  3. Enter Valid From and Valid To to cover the intended work dates. Use meaningful names such as the team, policy and effective period.
  4. Save. A different authorized HR user opens Attendance Assignment Review → Assignments, selects the row and enters the decision reason.
  5. Choose Approve selected assignment. Use Void selected assignment with a reason to retire an incorrect assignment through the controlled workflow.
  6. Run coverage for the date range and resolve missing or overlapping matches.

Roster resolution gives a temporary employee assignment precedence over an employee assignment, and an employee assignment precedence over an approved work-group default. Ambiguity at the winning level is blocked; the system does not choose randomly between competing assignments.

Employment Legal Regime Assignments

Record the employee or group, effective dates, regime code/name, applicability, legal source reference and rationale. Have the assignment independently reviewed. Applicability must come from the company’s HR/legal decision; the application does not infer a legal regime from a job title or nationality.

Delegations and fallback routes

  1. For a temporary supervisor absence, create Attendance Delegations with the supervisor, delegate and effective dates.
  2. An independent HR reviewer approves the delegation in Attendance Assignment Review → Supervisor delegations.
  3. If no usable supervisor or delegate exists, an authorized HR maker can create a sourced fallback route in the review form, scoped to a work group or organization and effective dates.
  4. A different HR reviewer approves the fallback route. The direct supervisor/delegation takes precedence; a work-group fallback is considered before an organization default.
  5. Recheck the reviewer on the actual request. Avoid self-approval and overlapping or stale routes.
Completion checkThe assigned reviewer is the intended person for the date and organization. Access rights and a routing record must both be valid.

Chapter 12 / 37

Prepare an attendance CSV

Who uses thisAttendance operator
OutcomeProduce a file that the custom importer can validate without ambiguity.

Use a UTF-8, comma-separated file containing exactly two columns: Code,AttendanceTime. Use one row per recorded punch. The optional first header line is recognized; including it is recommended. Each timestamp uses a 24-hour time and seconds: yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm:ss.

CodeAttendanceTime
Deepal2026-11-06 08:30:00
Deepal2026-11-06 12:30:00
Deepal2026-11-06 13:30:00
Deepal2026-11-06 17:30:00

Example only: Deepal must be the verified HR employee Code in the selected organization. These four synthetic punches illustrate an office day with a one-hour lunch. They are not evidence that this employee actually attended on this date.

  • Save as CSV UTF-8, not XLSX renamed to CSV. Use commas, not a regional semicolon delimiter.
  • Use the exact employee code. Avoid leading or trailing spaces in timestamps and avoid Excel serial dates or timezone suffixes.
  • Do not add employee names, IN/OUT labels, department columns or blank separator rows to this format.
  • Include complete actual punches for each workday. Missing or extra distinct punches are reviewed against the schedule.
  • Keep the file within 2 MiB. The default configured row limit is 5,000; administrators may configure a supported limit up to 20,000.
  • Split by work organization and a manageable date range. Preview also bounds the employee population and total employee-days.

Before upload

Confirm that the From date and To date cover the intended work dates, including the roster rules for an overnight shift. Review the company’s timezone. Keep the original export unchanged so later corrections can be traced to it.

Completion checkOpen the CSV in a plain text viewer and confirm the first few lines contain two values with exact timestamps. For demo files, select Synthetic demo data when uploading.

Upload and preview attendance

Who uses thisAttendance operator
OutcomeSave an upload and see which employee-days are ready or need attention.
  1. Open Attendance Import & Review and expand Upload settings.
  2. Select Work organization. Choose the organization containing the HR employee records for this file.
  3. Set From date and To date, and choose the attendance document type, for example the configured SLERPTECH Attendance type.
  4. For synthetic test punches, select Synthetic demo data — prevent supervisor submission.
  5. Choose Upload CSV and select the prepared file. Read any parsing error before continuing.
  6. Choose Preview / Refresh. Review the batch name, date range, version and summary.
  7. Use Resume recent upload in selected organization to reopen a saved batch later. Use New upload when starting a different file.

What preview compares

Preview resolves employee identity, active employment and organization, roster/policy coverage, the working day and breaks, holidays, approved leave, staged rows, saved punches and duplicate receipts. It also includes expected employees with no punches, subject to the selected population and date range.

Reading the grid

  • Each normal row represents an employee-day. Invalid source rows may appear as source groups rather than valid employee-days.
  • Use status, employee/code, department and issue-type filters to focus on exceptions. Page size controls the display, not the total import scope.
  • Click a column heading to sort. Resize column boundaries or use Fit columns where available. Use horizontal scrolling for long punch or validation text.
  • Click a row to open its detail pane. The grid can collapse to make room for source data, correction controls and history. Close details to return to the list.
  • Expand Batch overview and filters when you need the full counts or to change filters. Hover the small information icons for task help.
Completion checkThere is a current preview, the correct organization/date range, and understandable ready/attention/imported counts. Review the issue list before confirming any import.

Batch mapping

Select the unmatched code row, choose the verified employee under Map CSV code to employee, enter a correction/mapping reason, and choose Save mapping & revalidate. The mapping applies to this batch. Do not map several people’s codes to one employee to remove error counts.

Enabling a scheduled flow

  1. Validate the equivalent manual process on a bounded reviewed scope.
  2. Choose an active service user/role with the intended HR allowlist and organization/data access.
  3. Configure the explicit settings and limits. Keep disabled or blank values until reviewed.
  4. Create and activate the native scheduler job with the correct process and parameters; verify its identity and timezone.
  5. Run a controlled test, inspect its logs/outputs and the automation readiness report, then monitor subsequent runs.
  6. If it fails, resolve the source or access issue and retry through the supported flow. Do not enable broad privileges or create replacement punches to hide the failure.
Completion checkGeneration and recalculation create/refine review data only. Communications create/send notices under their settings. None of these scheduled flows grants authority to approve attendance or finalize payroll automatically.

Chapter 37 / 37

Acceptance checklist and support handover

Who uses thisHR owner and deployment reviewer
OutcomeConfirm the configured company workflow is ready for routine use.

Configuration acceptance

  • The installed JAR and dictionary package are compatible with this guide’s baseline or a reviewed later version.
  • Employee, supervisor, organization and role access are correct.
  • Roster, policy, calendar, legal applicability and assignment versions are reviewed and date-effective.
  • Grace, rounding, break, night/five-day and payable decisions have company references.
  • The actual holiday calendar and employee work population have been checked.

End to end acceptance

  • Valid CSV imports only once; duplicate retry creates no duplicate punches.
  • Unknown codes, bad timestamps, missing/extra punches and missing setup are highlighted.
  • A person with no punches appears in expected-day investigation.
  • Full/partial leave, rest days, holidays and overnight shifts have correct expectations.
  • Employee submission reaches the intended supervisor; self and unrelated decisions are blocked.
  • Corrections preserve originals and re-open required review; evidence controls work.
  • Overtime authorization and HR payable decisions are distinct.
  • Period close blocks unresolved work; eligible reopen and draft void paths reconcile.
  • Payroll handoff, independent completion and later compensation are traceable without duplicate quantities.
  • Reports, notices, assisted contact and any enabled schedules are understood by their owners.

What to include in a support case

Provide the screen name, employee code or approved internal identifier, organization, work date/range, upload batch or document number, exact error text, role name, action attempted and the relevant source/review status. Include the installed versions and whether the issue occurs after refresh/re-login. Use approved secure channels for evidence; never include passwords or unnecessary personal data.

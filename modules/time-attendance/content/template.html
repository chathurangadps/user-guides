Company policy and data handling

HR owns effective dates, legal applicability, break rules, night-work review, payable treatment and payroll mappings. This guide explains operation; it does not establish legal entitlements or authorize deductions.

Screens contain company/demo context. Use approved demo data for new screenshots and keep credentials and private employee evidence out of the guide.
